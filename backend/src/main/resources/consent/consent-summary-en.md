## In short

- We store your name and email address from your Google account, your dice rolls and notes, and the teams you choose
- Label photos are optional. Never photograph people
- Everyone taking part sees your name, your teams and which beers you tasted today, and your tasting notes without your name on the beer's page once three people have tasted it. The people you taste with see your rolls once everyone has rolled
- Administrators see more, to approve photos and catch cheating
- Old results from wiki.cantara.no show the names as they were written in the old spreadsheets. You can ask to have your name removed
- You can withdraw your consent at any time under Me, and ask for deletion by getting in touch with {contact}
- No tracking and no ads. Only what logging in needs
