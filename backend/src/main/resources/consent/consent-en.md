## Privacy and consent

Julebrus is Cantara's Christmas beer tasting. It is run by the Cantara community.

{sponsor} sponsors Cantara and pays for the hosting.

## What we store

- Your name and email address from your Google account
- Ratings and tasting notes, group sessions and duels. Badges are calculated from these and not stored
- A weight per season, which is calculated and stored for every participant
- Links to earlier seasons, the decisions about them and consent events, with timestamps
- Earlier seasons' ratings, imported from the public pages on wiki.cantara.no, with the names as written in the old spreadsheets, sometimes with an initial or surname
- Where the organisers have linked earlier ratings to an email address, that address is stored with them
- Pictures of beer labels that you choose to upload, with the time and the beer they belong to
- The teams you choose to play for
- Cheers and emojis you give in "I kveld", with timestamps
- Preferences (language and snow), kept in your browser's local storage

## Before you have answered

To log you in we store your Google name and email address the first time you sign in, before you have decided on consent. Nothing else about you is stored before consent, and no weight is calculated. If you decline and ask {contact} for deletion, we remove them.

## Why

To run the tasting and show results and history.

## Who sees what

- Members of a group see each other's scores when everyone in the group has rated, or when the group has been closed
- All participants see your name and your teams, and in "I kveld" which beers you have tasted today, which table you sit at and what you have cheered. Your dice are not shown there, only the day's average per beer once at least three people have rolled it
- Your tasting notes (appearance, aroma, taste, finish and tags) are shown on the beer's page to the other participants, without your name or the time and in random order, only once at least three people have tasted the beer and only to those who have tasted it themselves. Your comment and your dice are shown at the table only
- Season statistics are shown per beer
- The history shows the names of the people who tasted in earlier years, as written in the old spreadsheets
- Administrators see the name and email address of people who ask to link themselves to earlier seasons, in the approval queue
- Pictures you upload are seen only by you and the administrators until an administrator has approved them. After approval all participants can see them
- Administrators see every picture that is uploaded, and the name and email address of the person who uploaded it
- Administrators see every participant's name, email address, number of scores, weight and statistics on how they score (for example brewery preferences), to detect rigging

## Pictures of the label

- Pictures are optional. Photograph the label only, with no people in it
- You keep the copyright in your picture. You allow the tasting to show it to the other participants after an administrator has approved it
- Pictures are stored in our database, never as public links. Metadata such as location and camera is removed, and the picture is made smaller
- The label artwork in a picture belongs to the producer
- You can delete your own picture. You can also ask {contact} to delete it
- When you delete a picture, the picture itself is deleted. A small record (who, which beer and when) is kept to count uploads against the limits
- If you withdraw your consent, your pictures are no longer shown to the other participants

## Earlier seasons

The names from earlier seasons come from public wiki pages. You can ask for your name to be removed. You can also link your name to your login under Me to see your own old ratings. An administrator has to approve the link.

## Withdrawing and deletion

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the button under Me. That closes your access, and you cannot use the app until you consent again. It does not delete the data already stored. To have it deleted, get in touch with {contact}. The organisers then delete your data by hand.

## How long

We keep the data until you ask for deletion. Withdrawing consent only closes your access. The app uses no cookies beyond what login and security (CSRF) need.
