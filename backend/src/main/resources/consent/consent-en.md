## Privacy and consent

Julebrus is Cantara's Christmas beer tasting. It is run by the Cantara community. {sponsor} sponsors Cantara and pays for the hosting.

## What we store

- Your name and email address from your Google account
- Ratings and tasting notes, group sessions, duels and badges
- Earlier seasons' ratings, imported from the public pages on wiki.cantara.no, with the first names used there

## Why

To run the tasting and show results and history.

## Who sees what

- Members of a group see each other's scores after everyone has tasted the beer
- Season statistics are shown per beer
- The history shows the first names of the people who tasted in earlier years

## Earlier seasons

The names from earlier seasons come from public wiki pages. You can ask for your name to be removed. You can also link your name to your login under Me to see your own old ratings. An administrator has to approve the link.

## Withdrawing and deletion

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the button under Me. You then cannot use the app until you consent again. To have your data deleted, get in touch: {contact}.

## How long

We keep the data until you withdraw your consent or ask for deletion. The app uses no cookies beyond what login and security (CSRF) need.
